## 🏛️ 1. Sovereign Asset Recovery, Enforcement, and State Immunity
This domain examines the structural mechanisms, legal hurdles, and enforcement strategies involved in tracing, freezing, and recovering state assets across multiple international jurisdictions.
### Key Research Topics & Published References
- **Enforcing Sovereign Arbitral Awards & State Defences**
    - **Focus:** Analyzing the complexities of executing arbitral awards against sovereign assets under the doctrine of state immunity and creditor enforcement strategies in cross-border litigation.
    - **Academic Reference:** [_The Cambridge Handbook of Immunities and International Law_ (Cambridge University Press)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/cambridge-handbook-of-immunities-and-international-law/cambridge-handbook-of-immunities-and-international-law/3AB2F2100741518254FF64F32C6F6CC4?utm_source=gemini).

- **Cross-Border Illicit Finance & Asset Tracing Frameworks**
    - **Focus:** Investigating institutional capacities and modern legal protocols utilized by Asset Recovery Offices (AROs) to counter international financial crimes and recover state property.
    - **Academic Reference:** [_ASIL Insights_ (American Society of International Law)](https://asil.org/insights/?utm_source=gemini), examining international asset recovery mechanisms and transnational cooperation frameworks.
## ⚖️ 2. International Investment Law and Investor-State Dispute Settlement (ISDS)
This area focuses on the structural balance between state regulatory sovereignty and foreign investor protections under Bilateral Investment Treaties (BITs), as well as systemic challenges in arbitration.
### Key Research Topics & Published References
- **Arbitrating Corruption and Public Policy Defences in ISDS**
    - **Focus:** Evaluating how tribunals handle corruption allegations, the legality requirement under investment treaties, and the impact on arbitral jurisdiction and state liability.
    - **Academic Reference:** [Rachel Brewster, _Arbitrating Corruption_, Duke Law Scholarship Repository](https://scholarship.law.duke.edu/faculty_scholarship/4294/?utm_source=gemini).
    
- **The Intersection of UNCAC and Investor-State Disputes**
    - **Focus:** Integrating anti-corruption standards (such as the UN Convention against Corruption) into investment treaty frameworks and examining state responsibility.
    - **Academic Reference:** [Prof. Yarik Kryvoi's Research & Publications on Corruption and ISDS at BIICL](https://kryvoi.net/international-investment-law.html?utm_source=gemini).
## 🗺️ 3. Comparative Jurisprudence and Regulatory Harmonization
This research explores the cross-system interactions between civil law and common law traditions, statutory harmonization, and the practical application of international conventions in global commerce.
### Key Research Topics & Published References
- **Legal Enforcement Gaps in Modern Commercial and Investment Regimes**
    - **Focus:** Bridging the gap between normative legal standards and practical enforcement realities in cross-border trade, digital investments, and contract compliance.
    - **Academic Reference:** Peer-reviewed studies on legal enforcement and regulatory compliance, such as frameworks published in [Kluwer Arbitration Blog on Economic Crimes & Investor-State Disputes](https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/arbitration-blog/bribery-corruption-and-fraud-in-investor-state-disputes-how-should-tribunals-approach-economic-crimes/?utm_source=gemini).
