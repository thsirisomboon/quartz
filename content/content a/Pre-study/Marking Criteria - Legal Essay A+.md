**This grade indicates work of exceptionally high quality which, taking into account the nature of the work (e.g. course work or exam) will demonstrate many, though not necessarily all, of the following elements : 
- Exceptional knowledge of the relevant material.
- Exceptional understanding of the relevant material in relation to the topic.
- Independent thought and critical thinking of very high quality and an individual and effective treatment of the issues, based on extensive reading.
- Highly advanced analysis of relevant primary and secondary sources.
- Excellent command of a coherent and well-developed, evidence - based, compelling argument across the whole piece of work.
- An excellent standard of writing (including spelling and grammar) and presentation (referencing, bibliography etc.).
    
# **A First class legal student**
Question : Close attention to the question and answering that question with breadth and accuracy.

Understanding : Detail knowledge of the topic addressed as well as a deeper understanding of the context in which that topic exists

Analysis : Presentation of theoretical arguments concerning the subject, significant critical analysis of relevant sources, and thoughtful personal perspective on the debates.

Communication : A well - developed and compelling argument coupled with excellent standard of writing.

Example : when the question asks “What is the constitution” ?
- Don’t use outline nature & sources of the constitution
- Don’t list the different categories of a constitution
- Find different definitions of constitution
- Identify the central features of a constitution**
