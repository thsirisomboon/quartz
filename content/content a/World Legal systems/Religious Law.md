









- ### **Illustrative Jurisdictions**
- Islamic Law (Sharia): Countries where Islamic law is the primary source: Saudi Arabia, Iran. Countries with Islamic law integrated into civil systems: #Egypt , #Pakistan , #Sudan , #Indonesia , #Malaysia.
- Jewish Law (Halakha): Used in religious courts in Israel for personal status matters (marriage, divorce, etc.).
- Hindu Law: Applies to personal law (e.g., marriage and inheritance) in #India and #Nepal, alongside civil law.
